Circular-shift tests for the specified traits and significant/combined TOGA dog sets. The observed mean chromosome/SNP-count-stratified signal-rank percentile is compared with 10,000 within-chromosome shifts of gene-set membership. Smaller within-stratum rank percentile means stronger association. Empirical p is one-sided with a plus-one correction and no multiple-testing adjustment.

Rows: 8. Tab-separated UTF-8 text with one header row. Numeric values retain computational precision rather than notebook display rounding. Blank fields indicate missing or inapplicable values.

Columns
Trait: Human GWAS trait (OCD, CAD, BIP, ADHD, MDD or SCZ).
Window: TSS window: 1mb is ±1 Mb; 500kb is ±500 kb, inclusive.
Dog set: Dog set; see the table context above.
Dog genes: Number of covered genes in the selected dog subset.
Mean rank: Observed mean chromosome/SNP-count-stratified signal-rank percentile; smaller means stronger association.
Null mean rank: Mean selected-set signal-rank percentile across the circular-shift null replicates.
Empirical p: One-sided circular-shift p-value with a plus-one correction, testing smaller mean rank.
Permutations: Number of within-chromosome circular-shift replicates.
