Circular-shift enrichment tests for the 48 ST6 Human genes. Each trait/window uses 10,000 independent within-chromosome circular shifts of gene-set membership among covered genes ordered by genomic position. The statistic is the mean signal-rank percentile within chromosome × SNP-count strata (smaller is stronger), rather than the genome-wide ranks used in CDFs. Empirical one-sided p-values use a plus-one correction. These p-values are unadjusted.

Rows: 6. Tab-separated UTF-8 text with one header row. Numeric values retain computational precision rather than notebook display rounding. Blank fields indicate missing or inapplicable values.

Columns
Trait: Human GWAS trait (OCD, CAD, BIP, ADHD, MDD or SCZ).
Window: TSS window: 1mb is ±1 Mb; 500kb is ±500 kb, inclusive.
Human genes: Number of covered ST6 Human genes.
Mean rank: Observed mean chromosome/SNP-count-stratified signal-rank percentile; smaller means stronger association.
Null mean rank: Mean selected-set signal-rank percentile across the circular-shift null replicates.
Empirical p: One-sided circular-shift p-value with a plus-one correction, testing smaller mean rank.
Shifts: Number of within-chromosome circular-shift replicates.
