Contents
January 2022 ADHD GWAS meta-analysis of iPSYCH, deCODE and PGC data. Gzip-compressed table with CHR, SNP, BP, A1, A2, frequencies, INFO, OR, SE, P, Direction, Nca and Nco. The source PDF specifies BP positions on hg19/GRCh37. The publisher README uses a different display filename (ADHD_meta_Jan2022_iPSYCH1_iPSYCH2_deCODE_PGC.meta.gz), but its MD5 matches this file.
