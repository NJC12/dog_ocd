Covered gene counts for every human GWAS trait and TSS window. Covered means at least one valid SNP within the inclusive ±1 Mb or ±500 kb window. Covered dog genes are members of the 83-gene TOGA set; non-dog genes are the remaining covered genes.

Rows: 12. Tab-separated UTF-8 text with one header row. Numeric values retain computational precision rather than notebook display rounding. Blank fields indicate missing or inapplicable values.

Columns
Trait: Human GWAS trait (OCD, CAD, BIP, ADHD, MDD or SCZ).
Window: TSS window: 1mb is ±1 Mb; 500kb is ±500 kb, inclusive.
Covered genes: Number of protein-coding genes with at least one valid SNP in this window.
Covered dog genes: Number of covered genes in the 83-gene TOGA dog set.
Non-dog genes: Number of background genes: non-dog for dog tests, all other genes for Human-set tests.
